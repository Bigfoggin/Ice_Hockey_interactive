# Brainstorming Ideas

**Research Question: How can we transform real performance data in ice hockey into an experience that people actually want to explore?**

The goal is to develop a *simple*, interactive, and entertaining web experience. This should help both ice hockey experts and beginners playfully understand what is happening on the ice and why a player's performance was important using real match data (from the Spengler Cup) – *instead of just showing dry tables of numbers*.

# Alessio

## 1. Dynamic Performance Star Chart

* **Real-time radar chart:** Visualizes attack, defense, speed, synergy, and morale during live play.
* **Baseline comparison:** Overlays current match stats against the team's historical averages.
* **Data-driven commentary:** Highlights strategic shifts, tactical adjustments, or performance drops in real time.

---

## 2. Speed & Pass Tracking

* **F1-style speedometer:** Displays puck velocity on shots and passes.
* **Color-coded outcomes:** **Green** for completed passes and goals; **Red** for turnovers and interceptions.

---

## 3. Shot Impact Metric

* **Impact force gauge:** Displays real-time shot power and stick flex intensity.

---

## 4. F1-Style Live Leaderboards

* **Corner display tower:** Positioned on the top-left of the screen.
* **Rotating metrics:** Alternates between top scorers, intercepted passes, key defensive plays, and passing accuracy.

---

## 5. Psychological & Effort Analytics

* **Behavioral insights:** Tracks variations in shot power relative to game context.
* **Emotion & pressure visualization:** Correlates spikes in shot force while trailing to frustration, urgency, or aggressive play style changes.
